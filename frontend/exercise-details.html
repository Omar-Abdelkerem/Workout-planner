Barbell Bench Press

Targeted muscle: Chest, Shoulders, Triceps

Instruction Steps:

  1. Lie back on a flat bench.
  2. Use a medium-width grip.
  3. Lower the bar to your chest.
  4. Push bar back up.

Safety Tips:

  • Keep feet flat on floor.
  • Use a spotter.
  • Do not bounce the bar.
